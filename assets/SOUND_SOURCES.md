# Squeak sources

The three `Squeak_*.mp3` files are distinct free recordings downloaded from
Mixkit's free sound-effects catalog under the Mixkit License:

- `Squeak_RubberDuck.mp3`: Rubber duck squeak, item 1014
- `Squeak_Little.mp3`: Little squeak, item 1018

Source page: https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/squeak/
